# Your account

Open the **account menu** from the avatar in the top-right corner, then choose **Profile &amp;
settings** to manage your ZeroVDI sign-in.

## Changing your password

In **Profile &amp; settings**, open the password section, enter your current password and your new
password twice, and save. Use a strong, unique password.

If you sign in through your organisation's single sign-on, your password lives with your identity
provider, not with ZeroVDI — change it there.

### Forgotten it?

Use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page; a reset link is e-mailed to you. An administrator can
also send you one, or set a new password directly — ask them if the e-mail doesn't arrive.

## Two-factor authentication (2FA)

Two-factor authentication adds a one-time code on top of your password, so your account stays safe
even if your password leaks. Your administrator may **require** it — if so, you'll be guided to set it
up at sign-in and can't skip it.

There are two ways to receive the code, and **either one satisfies the requirement**. Set up both if
you'd like a fallback.

### Authenticator app (recommended)

1. Go to **Profile &amp; settings → Two-factor authentication → Add authenticator app**.
2. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app (Microsoft Authenticator, Google Authenticator,
   1Password, …).
3. Enter the 6-digit code the app shows to confirm.
4. **Save your recovery codes** somewhere safe — they let you back in if you lose your phone.

It keeps working with no signal and no connection, which is why it is the better of the two.

### Email code

1. Go to **Profile &amp; settings → Two-factor authentication → Enable email authentication**.
2. A code is sent to your account's e-mail address; enter it to confirm.

At sign-in a fresh code is sent automatically, and the page has a **Resend** link. Codes expire after
**five minutes**. If you have both methods, the sign-in page lets you switch between them.

### Lost your authenticator?

Use one of your saved recovery codes to sign in, then re-set up 2FA. If you enabled email codes too,
use those instead. If you've lost everything, contact your administrator — they can reset your
account.

> If you sign in through your organisation's single sign-on, two-factor is normally handled there and
> ZeroVDI won't ask you a second time.

## Theme

The **palette icon** in the top bar lets you switch color theme and light/dark mode. If your
administrator has locked the appearance, some or all of these choices may be hidden.
