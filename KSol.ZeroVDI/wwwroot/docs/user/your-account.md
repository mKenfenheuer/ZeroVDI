# Your account

Open the **account menu** from the avatar in the top-right corner, then choose **Profile &amp;
settings** to manage your ZeroVDI sign-in.

## Changing your password

In **Profile &amp; settings**, open the password section, enter your current password and your new
password twice, and save. Use a strong, unique password.

## Two-factor authentication (2FA)

Two-factor authentication adds a one-time code on top of your password, so your account stays safe
even if your password leaks. Your administrator may **require** it — if so, you'll be guided to set it
up at sign-in and can't skip it.

To set it up yourself:

1. Go to **Profile &amp; settings → two-factor / authenticator**.
2. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app (Microsoft Authenticator, Google Authenticator, 1Password, …).
3. Enter the 6-digit code the app shows to confirm.
4. **Save your recovery codes** somewhere safe — they let you back in if you lose your phone.

From then on, you'll enter a code from the app each time you sign in.

### Lost your authenticator?

Use one of your saved recovery codes to sign in, then re-set up 2FA. If you've lost those too,
contact your administrator to reset your account.

## Theme

The **palette icon** in the top bar lets you switch color theme and light/dark mode. If your
administrator has locked the appearance, some or all of these choices may be hidden.
